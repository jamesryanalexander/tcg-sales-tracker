Import a TCGPlayer orders CSV

From your TCGPlayer Seller Portal, go to Orders, select the orders you want, and use Export Shipping to download a CSV. Upload it here.

Column names vary by export; whatever this importer can't find (most commonly per-order marketplace fees) is left blank and the order is flagged needs review so you can fill it in by hand afterward. Re-uploading the same export is safe — orders are matched by order number and never duplicated.
